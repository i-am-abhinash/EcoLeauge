#  Ecolauge – Gamified Environmental Education Platform

##  Overview
Ecolauge is a gamified environmental education platform designed to promote awareness about sustainability and environmental protection.  
The system provides interactive learning experiences to engage students and encourage eco-friendly practices.

---

##  Problem Statement
Environmental awareness among students is often limited due to lack of engaging learning methods.  
Traditional education methods fail to create interest and long-term impact.

Ecolauge addresses this by introducing gamification into environmental education.

---

##  Features
- Interactive learning modules on environmental topics  
- Gamification elements (points, levels, rewards)  
- Engaging and user-friendly interface  
- Focus on real-world environmental issues  
- Designed for students and educational institutions
